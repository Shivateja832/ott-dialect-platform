# Known limitations and external validation gates

1. **Challenge pack and live replay are absent.** The retained 279-line brief was reviewed, but the 30-day event/profile exports, full catalogue files, 12-rule goal catalogue, intervention catalogue, past-pilot data, and timed replay script are not in the project. The CLI accepts a normalized JSON pack; exact mapping and replay certification remain blocked until those materials are supplied.
2. **Goal definitions are not fabricated.** The engine is data-driven and can evaluate more than the two example rules in the generated fixture. The actual 12 definitions were not supplied. Two rules are described as underspecified and one success signal is absent; rules must be explicitly marked underspecified or disabled when their signal is absent from `eventTypes`.
3. **Replay delivery is simulated by design.** The challenge explicitly permits mock/replay mode. Push, WhatsApp, and in-app actions are recorded as `SIMULATED` after consent, local quiet-hour, template, timezone, and daily-cap checks; no real message is sent.
4. **Synthetic message policy data.** The generated fixture uses synthetic consent, timezone, and approved-template records to exercise the required policy checks; those records represent test data, not real viewers.
5. **Single-process persistence.** JSONL logs and per-viewer JSON snapshots are durable for local replay, but are not a transactional multi-writer store. Distributed locking, database failover, retention, encryption at rest, backup, access controls, and schema migrations need production infrastructure.
6. **Synthetic outcomes are not impact evidence.** The configured outcome simulator validates the reporting pipeline only. It does not estimate real-world treatment effects. Actual pilot and live-replay data were unavailable.
7. **Measurement precision.** The report uses an intention-to-treat randomized comparison and approximate normal interval. It does not perform covariate adjustment, sequential-testing correction, contamination analysis, or full experiment power analysis.
8. **Rule refresh is trigger-driven.** Corrected rules refresh an active plan the next time that viewer triggers. No proactive affected-viewer index or scheduled deadline worker is implemented.
9. **Load results are local and synthetic.** `npm run load-test` tests a 1,000-viewer burst; `npm run load-test:sustained` completed 18,000 generated opens at 30/second for ten minutes on this machine (p95 711 ms, max 4.38 seconds). Neither result certifies a Render plan or production database/provider capacity.
10. **Hosted replay demo.** The synthetic replay app is deployed at [ott-dialect-platform-demo.onrender.com](https://ott-dialect-platform-demo.onrender.com) and its health endpoint is verified. Exact challenge-pack validation is not possible because the challenge data and timed replay were not supplied.
